FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What kind of tools the application offers?
BRET focuses on simple tools that work here and now. You can use breathing, relaxation, and calming audio to feel better in just a few minutes.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Your data is stored securely. We follow GDPR standards and never share your personal
information.
How can I test the application?
The app is free to use right now. You can try all exercises without limits. If you like it, you can
support our team to help us grow the product.
Do I need special equipment?
No. Just your phone and internet connection.
How long does one session take?
Most sessions take 3–10 minutes.
Is the app available offline?
Not yet. For now, BRET works only online.
Will you add more exercises in the future?
Yes! BRET will grow with new features like “Shake Off” (stress-release inspired by animals), meditations, and more. BRET is also the first step toward our larger app EmTalk.

Who this app is for

For office workers

Long hours, deadlines, and heavy tasks can hurt your focus and well-being. BRET gives you
quick breathing and relaxation exercises you can do anytime

How it helps:

Take a 5-minute break to recharge
Reduce stress before important meetings
Stay focused during long work sessions

For students

Exams, deadlines, and busy schedules can be stressful. BRET helps you relax and improve
focus with short, guided breathing practices.

How it helps:

Calm your mind before exams
Recover energy between classes
Build healthy study habits

For people with anxiety

Living with stress and anxiety can feel overwhelming. BRET offers soothing audio, guided

How it helps:

Manage stress in the moment
