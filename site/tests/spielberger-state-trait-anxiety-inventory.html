Is this the right starting point?

You want a broader look at anxiety, rather than a focus on social situations alone.

What can I take away?

A way to reflect on the difference between your current feelings and your usual experience.

This is a self-assessment, not a diagnosis.

Before you start. For questions 1–20, answer how you feel right now. For questions 21–40, answer how you generally feel.
