Is this the right starting point?

You are reflecting on what you value at work, rather than looking for a mental health assessment.

What can I take away?

A prompt to think about your preferences and what you would like from your work.

This is a self-assessment, not a diagnosis.

Before you start. Read each question carefully. Where indicated, select one or two answers; some questions allow several answers.
