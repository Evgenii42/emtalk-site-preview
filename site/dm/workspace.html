How this works

Start with one to eight options. With up to three, begin with pros and cons. With four or more, choose between every pair, then select up to three options to explore in more depth.

Pairwise points count your choices. Earlier saved files may include undecided pairs, which add no points. Reasons use importance weights of 1, 3, 5 and 8. Balanced reasons start at 50; stronger reasons move the score toward 0 or 100 using the DM app's confidence curve.

Scores reflect what you entered. They are not probabilities, guarantees or instructions about what you should choose. Comparison and pros-and-cons results are separate exercises.

AI assistance is available in the app. Coming soon to the website. Get DM on Google Play. Coming soon to the App Store.
