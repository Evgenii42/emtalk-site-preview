EmTalk Privacy Policy

Effective Date:
January 22, 2026

Welcome to EmTalk  a digital ecosystem designed to support clarity, better decision-making, and personal growth while respecting your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how it is handled. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have regarding your data when using the EmTalk website.

‍

Table of Contents

This Privacy Policy is presented in a layered format. Each section contains a brief summary for convenience, followed by detailed explanations. We encourage you to review the full policy to understand how your information is collected, used, and protected when using Decision Matrix (DM).Sections of this Privacy Policy include:

  2. Use of Personal Information

1. Collection of Personal Information

We collect the following categories of personal information when you use the EmTalk website:

Information you provide:
Name, email address, and any information you choose to submit through forms (such as feedback, survey responses, waitlist sign-ups, or messages).

Form submissions (Google Forms):
Some data is collected through Google Forms. This may include your responses, contact details, and any additional information you voluntarily provide.

Usage data:
Information about how you interact with the website, including pages visited, session duration, device type, browser type, and interaction events. This data is collected automatically and is used in aggregated or anonymized form where possible.

Optional information:
You may choose to provide additional information (such as preferences or context about your use case). Providing this information is not required.

‍

‍

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and messages
  • To process form submissions (e.g. feedback, waitlists, or requests)
  • To improve website content, tools, and user experience
  • To understand user needs and prioritize future features
  • To send updates or communications, only when you have provided your email for that purpose
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

Analytics and infrastructure:
EmTalk may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to understand how the website is used, improve performance, and enhance user experience. Data is processed in aggregated or anonymized form where possible.

Advertising:
EmTalk does not display ads and does not use personal data for advertising purposes.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, request your consent.

‍

3. Sharing of Personal Information

EmTalk does not sell your personal information.

We may share personal data only with:

  • Service providers that help operate the website (such as hosting, analytics, or form processing providers like Google), under confidentiality obligations
  • Legal authorities, when required by law
  • Business partners, in connection with a merger or acquisition, with appropriate safeguards

All third parties are expected to protect your data.

‍

4. Data Security & Retention

We take your privacy seriously. EmTalk uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data.

While no system can guarantee absolute security, we continuously improve our safeguards.

Personal data is retained:

  • for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above
  • or as required by applicable law

You may request deletion of your data at any time.

‍

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct your information
  • Request data deletion
  • Restrict or object to certain data uses
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise your rights, contact us at:
📩 info@emtalk.org

We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

‍

6. Children’s Privacy

The EmTalk website is not intended for individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that such data has been provided, we will take steps to delete it.

If you believe a minor has shared personal information with us, please contact us.

‍

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

EmTalk may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • understand usage patterns
  • improve performance
  • ensure basic website functionality

These technologies are not used for advertising purposes.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

The website may also include forms or call-to-action elements (such as sign-ups or feedback requests). These are not personalized using personal data unless you explicitly provide it.

‍

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of the date indicated above.

We may update it from time to time to reflect changes to the website, legal requirements, or improvements in clarity.

Material changes may be communicated through the website or other appropriate channels.

‍9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at:

📩 info@emtalk.org

We aim to respond in a timely and transparent manner.

‍

10. Supplemental Notices

EmTalk is available worldwide.

Your personal data may be processed in Kazakhstan or in other countries where our service providers operate.

By using the website, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to jurisdictions with different data protection standards. Where required, we apply appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as:

  • GDPR / UK GDPR
  • CCPA / CPRA
  • PIPEDA

We comply with applicable data protection laws.

We comply with applicable data protection laws in the jurisdictions where DM is offered. If you have questions about international data transfers or your regional rights, please contact us at:

📩 info@emtalk.org
