Delete Your Account and Associated Data

Effective Date:
January 22, 2026

If you no longer wish to interact with EmTalk or want your data removed, you can request deletion of your personal data at any time.

How to request data deletion

Since EmTalk currently does not require account registration on the website, data deletion is handled via request.

To request deletion of your data:

📩 Email us at: info@emtalk.org

Please include:

  • The email address you used (if applicable)
  • A clear request for data deletion

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request.

What data may be deleted

Upon request, we will delete personal data associated with you, including:

  • Contact information (such as name and email address)
  • Form submissions (e.g. feedback, waitlists, survey responses)
  • Any other information you have provided to us

Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests within 7–14 days, unless a shorter timeframe is required by applicable law.

What data may be retained

Certain data may be retained for a limited period where necessary for legal or operational purposes:

  • Anonymous technical logs (for security and system monitoring)
  • Records required for legal compliance

All retained data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Depending on your location (including the EU / EEA), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw consent

Need help?

If you have questions about your data or privacy:

📩 info@emtalk.org

We aim to respond in a timely and transparent manner.
