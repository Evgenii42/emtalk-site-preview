WHY TAKE A TEST?

A clearer starting point for understanding yourself

These tests turn vague feelings into something easier to reflect on. They can help you recognize recurring patterns, understand their intensity, and decide whether rest, a conversation, or professional support may be useful.

01

Notice patterns

See how often certain thoughts, emotions.

02

Find the words

Use the result as a prompt for journaling or a more focused conversation.

03

Choose a next step

Receive practical guidance based on your answers and current situation.

Important: self-assessment tests are educational tools, not a medical diagnosis. If symptoms are severe, persistent, or affect daily life, consider speaking with a qualified professional.
